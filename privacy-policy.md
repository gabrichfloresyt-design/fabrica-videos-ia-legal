# Privacy Policy — Fábrica de Videos IA

**Last updated:** October 18, 2026

Fábrica de Videos IA ("the App") is a private desktop application operated by **OSI TECH** ("we", "us"). It runs
only on the operator's own computer. It is used to create short videos and to publish them on the operator's own social
media accounts. The App is not offered to the public, and no other people sign up for or use it.

Contact: **ositech_777@yahoo.com**

## 1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses **YouTube API Services** to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and to check their status.
By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: <https://www.youtube.com/t/terms>.
Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## 2. Other platforms we connect to

The App also uses the official APIs of:

- **Meta** (Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts), via Facebook Login.
  Meta Privacy Policy: <https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/>.
- **TikTok**, via Login Kit and the Content Posting API.
  TikTok Privacy Policy: <https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy>.

Each connection is authorized explicitly by the account owner through the platform's own official login screen.

## 3. What information the App accesses, stores and shares

When the account owner connects an account, the App receives **only** what it needs to publish:

| Data | Why | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Access and refresh tokens issued by Google, Meta or TikTok | To upload and publish the operator's videos and check their status | Encrypted in the macOS Keychain of the operator's computer |
| Basic account identity: channel, Page or account ID and display name | To make sure each video goes to the correct account | Local database on the operator's computer |
| Publication results: post or video ID, link and status | To show what was published and avoid duplicates | Local database on the operator's computer |

The App does **not**:

- read other people's data, comments, messages, followers or analytics;
- sell, rent or share any data with third parties;
- use any data for advertising;
- send any data to servers of ours, because we have none: everything stays on the operator's computer.

The only data sent to each platform are the video and the texts (title, description, hashtags) that the operator
chooses to publish there.

## 4. Cookies

This website and the App do not use cookies or tracking technologies.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account remains connected. Local publication records are kept until the operator
deletes them.

To delete your data:

- **From the App:** press **"Desconectar"** (Disconnect) on the account. The token is deleted from the Keychain
  immediately.
- **Google / YouTube:** revoke access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions> (Google security
  settings).
- **Meta (Facebook / Instagram):** remove the App at **Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business
  integrations** (or **Apps and websites**).
- **TikTok:** remove the App at **TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services**
  (or **Manage app permissions**).
- **By email:** write to **ositech_777@yahoo.com** with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete every record related to
  your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

## 6. Security

Tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain and are never written to files, logs or exports. All connections to the
platforms use HTTPS.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
